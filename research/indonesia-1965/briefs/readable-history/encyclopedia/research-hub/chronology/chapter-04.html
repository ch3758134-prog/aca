Surviving PKI leaders: 1966 self-criticism, 1967 Sudisman — 2026-11-08

该段6项可定位日期记录

1965 1965 Aug–Sep as recalled July1967: Sudisman says Aidit told Politburo members officers planned preemptive action and dispatched few party cadres to regions with RRI-support brief; he says national Party Central Committee did NOT adopt operative military plan. His own acceptance of political responsibility doesn't establish institutional command. [S260,C391–C393]

1965 17 Aug 1965, as cited retrospectively in September 1966: the surviving Politburo’s self-criticism says a statement urged the revolutionary situation to its “peak”; it accuses leaders of overstating peasant/mass action and discounting contrary district reports. This is the 1966 faction’s causal diagnosis, not the recovered 1965 statement. ANRI KOTI item 507 is a matching catalogue lead with no date or text; Roosa separately reproduces a Harian Rakjat cartoon from 17 Aug 1965, not the Politburo statement. [S228;S252, printed pp.237–238;S262, printed pp.166–167;S1331;C2622–C2624]

1966 Sep 1966: the title leaf in the 1971 scan names a Politburo self-criticism from Central Java. It says complete analysis was difficult under “white terror” and asks Party members to help complete it. It calls the 1951 legal/parliamentary turn basically correct under the conditions then, while blaming the later failure to settle a principal struggle form and the fixing of peaceful legality as a principle. It criticizes land and labor strategy and advocates armed agrarian revolution that must grow through peasant struggle, not be imposed from outside. These are claims and prescriptions in a post-defeat text, not independently verified national practice. [S261;S262, printed pp.91–97, 112–127, 166–167;S1331;C2615–C2622]

1967 21 Jul 1967: Sudisman tells the military tribunal that the titled self-criticism was his effort and proposes appending it to his defense. This supports personal drafting/implementation involvement; the 1971 title leaf attributes it to the Politburo and no draft proves sole authorship. [S1339, lines 447–448;S260;C2614]

1968 Jan issue no.3, date reported in 1968 publisher note; Sep 1971: the Foreign Languages Press edition describes the 17 Aug 1966 statement and September self-criticism as separate texts, and reports the self-criticism had appeared in the January issue no.3; the original issue is absent. The Delegation’s 1971 edition reprints the text five years after its stated 1966 date and adds a later foreword. Keep reprint framing distinct from the 1966 body. [S261;S262;C2625–C2626]

1971 1971: a self-described Delegation of the CC PKI publishes the five-document collection; its foreword frames a later armed/rural strategy and gives inconsistent years for a May 23 message. The Delegation’s authority and the message’s date remain to be established from original copies. [S262,C399–C400,C2626]
