1962 1962–1966 — Soge/Ilir (Indramayu, West Java): Safitri distinguishes early Surat Prioritas recipient letters from the later formal SK LR.323/D/VIII/65/1966 dated 13 September 1966. The scholarly transcript says 283.37ha / 622 intended recipients / typically 0.35ha each; these numbers do not multiply to the entire project area, so do not treat 622 × 0.35ha as its total. The issuing document provided conditional rights, with a 15-year payment schedule, first payment Rp6,600 and administrative Rp396, land-office registration, personal cultivation, membership of regional farmers' cooperative and bar on sale/transfer before ownership conditions were met. Article footnote30 nevertheless says the 1966 decree copy was received in 1964 — preserve unresolved chronology; original instruments not examined. [S287; C474; C477; C485]

1965 1965 and aftermath — Soge: Witnesses interviewed in 2000 reported a local head (kuwu) and subdistrict chief (camat) used requests for priority land documents and PKI accusations against both party-affiliated and non-affiliated residents; this is oral-history testimony, not a verified full village imprisonment census. [S287;C475;C476]

1970 1970s–1995 — Soge: Safitri's reconstruction using LBH Bandung and an Indramayu land-office 1991 investigation reports renewed seizures of papers, pressure to sell, loss of cultivation access from about 1978, and failed attempts to recover documents. Her quoted 1991 sample traces at least 30 former recipients and about 15 further linked parcels into four owners holding 16ha; this is a traced subset, not all 622 allotments. Exact registry and deed files still needed. [S287;C486;C487]

2026-11-09 continuous legal-institutional follow-up (ONGOING)

该段10项可定位日期记录

Read 1965 Desapraja UU19 and 1979 Village Government UU5 OCR statutory articles alongside official BPK law metadata: 1965 art9/17 direct village-head and representative election, 10–25 assembly seats and head chairmanship; 1979 art4 explicitly disqualifies village-head candidates connected to G30S/PKI, art17 LMD composed of local officials/community leaders rather than direct election, and art10 places responsibility upwards via camat. This is a normative state-power mechanism, not proof of implementation in Soge/Wangunwati. 1969 UU6 art2 conditionally deferred repeal for Annex III statutes; 1979 law explanation links its replacement to this mechanism; Annex III original image still unread.

Confirmed 1973 Unit Desa -> 1978 BUUD/KUD -> 1984 KUD -> 1998 cooperative instruction changes via official BPK status; actual local cooperative control, rice purchases, credit and members' rights unverified. 1965/1967/1992 cooperative laws and 2014 Constitutional Court invalidation of 2012 law also verified at official legal-status/operative-decision level.
