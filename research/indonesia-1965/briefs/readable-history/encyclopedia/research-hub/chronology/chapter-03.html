Twelfth research pass — 1967-69 economic law and external aid material chain (2026-11-08)

该段7项可定位日期记录

1967 10 January 1967: UU1/1967 formally establishes FDI entry, Indonesian company form and licensing, conditions on managers and Indonesian workers, estate/building land rights, permitted dividend remittances, nationalisation compensation and mixed capital. Text describes law, not licence implementation. [S158/C159-C162]

1967 20–23 February 1967: Rostow requested conditional US aid approval up to $85m for 1967, $36m previously committed and one-third donor formula. Presidential note later approves if Japan/European two-thirds obtained, not delivery. [S159/C163]

1967 6–7 July 1967: US embassy recounts Suharto warning reduced dollar import loans or inappropriate PL480 commodities threatened his budget; asked for rice and opposed finished textiles harming Indonesian spinners/weavers. Embassy account not Indonesian verbatim. [S160/C164-C165]

1967 4 August 1967: US State NSC paper credits Suharto economists with import/liberalization, state-enterprise subsidy and utility charge changes, and urges more private FDI, international agency and donor aid with lower-profile US involvement. This is interested U.S. policy advocacy. [S161/C166-C167]

1967 17–21 November 1967: Rostow asked Johnson to approve US 1968 one-third share of anticipated $325m consortium requirement up to $110m; editorial note records Johnson's approval. [S162/C168]

1968 18 June 1968: Rostow sought US aid package $110m core (rice, cotton, AID loan) plus proposed $46m experimental wheat outside consortium formula; note records president checked requested action. Shipment unverified in this memo. [S163/C169-C170]

1969 1 April 1969 / 10 April approval: Rogers retrospective says 1967 US contribution $65.2m, 1968 $110m with wheat extra part shipped, and recounts bilateral Western and Soviet creditor reschedulings, IMF missions and World Bank Jakarta office. New 1969 pledge conditional, Nixon later approved program per source note. [S164/C171-C173]

Thirteenth-pass 1965 Southeast Asian relations addendum — 2026-11-08

该段6项可定位日期记录

时期未定 3 March: Hasluck assessed Army worry about PKI influence and Confrontation military risk, but no evidence Army leadership had settled on peace with Malaysia. [S169;C174]

时期未定 11 November: Singapore-based Pritchett described barter losses >M$100m yearly as his assessment and strong Singapore pressure to resume trade with Indonesia; Canberra's direct same-day instruction urged Malaysia and Singapore to avoid a clash. [S166-S167;C178-C179]

时期未定 12–16 November: Ghazali met Indonesian officer Sukendro in Bangkok on Nov12, per Australian document's notes. Critchley reported Tunku's story of Sukendro seeking M$3m mainly medicines and suggesting indirect Western support via Malaysia, not a delivery record. [S165;C175-C176]

时期未定 18 November: Tunku told Critchley he would use Malaysian Navy against Indonesia ships if Singapore resumed barter, while Malaysian-controlled Tawau trade was exempt; he planned a Ghazali Bangkok message to Sukendro. Threat not known executed. [S168;C180-C181]

时期未定 26 November: DFAT editor's later note reports Ghazali saying they were ready to route goods not cash via Bangkok and Hong Kong and ensure delivery to Jakarta; no actual consignee/receipt found. [S165;C177]

1964 1964 Dec–1965 Oct UK IRD: Hall 2025 quotes/locates FO1110/2356 political-intent letters and reported Indonesian newsletter Kenjataan2 circulation; these remain historian-mediated UK source leads, not primary correspondence read independently here. [S170;C183-C184]

Fifteenth-pass chronological addendum: Nov1965–Oct1966

该段9项可定位日期记录

1965 10–12 Nov 1965: US Embassy 1425 notes Sukarno's renewed public political pressure and reported transfer of radio supervision back to civilian appointee; its second-hand Makassar and Bone claims cannot be counted as independently verified deaths/property proportions. [S190; C207–C209]

1965 15–19 Dec 1965: Singapore/Malaysia agreed on two 200-ton offshore barter vessels, but first Dec17 trade not verified by shipping records. Tunku told Hasluck the approx three-million medicine request from Indonesian Army remained undecided; he was unsure of currency, despite Nov reference to Malaysian dollars. [S181–S182; S165; C210–C213]

1965 17 Dec 1965: A-398 assembled PKI senior leadership list (95 positions, 67 people) and fragmentary whereabouts, expressly noting false confessions. An Aug1966 US message retrospectively acknowledged supplying a sanitized version to Indonesian government in Dec, with apparent security use. [S189; S032; C203–C205]

1966 19 Apr 1966: Singapore's Rajaratnam told Australian interlocutors Malik sought to end confrontation for economic rehabilitation; attributed Singapore-channel account. [S183; C214]

1966 31 May–1 Jun 1966: Bangkok negotiation produced preliminary Indonesia/Malaysia normalization framework subject to confirmation. [S184; C215]

1966 8–9 Jun 1966: Lee publicly indicated Singapore would coordinate recognition of Indonesia with Malaysia. [S193; C219]

1966 5–6 Aug 1966: Australian diplomatic reporting followed proposed secret letters about Borneo electoral reaffirmation as a condition of diplomatic recognition. [S185–S186; C216]

1966 11 Aug 1966: Adam Malik and Razak signed treaty in JAKARTA: Sabah/Sarawak electoral reaffirmation, restoring diplomatic relations, and cessation of hostility. [S187; C217–C218]

1966 27 Oct 1966: Sukarno signed Indonesian UU No5 ratifying Aug11 accord. Formal presidency continued despite altered actual military/political power. [S187; C217]

Sixteenth research tranche — oil assets, trade unions and production organization, 1960–1973 (2026-11-08)

该段15项可定位日期记录

1960 1960: Indonesian Petroleum Law No44 reserved legal control of oil and gas to state enterprises, requiring foreign operators to work under state authority. UN contract survey read; signed law and original texts still pending. [S197; C222]

1960 1960–64: Sukarno-era labor organization was contested. His July1960 union consolidation attempt met SOBSI opposition, while Army-associated BKS regrouped as SOKSI Dec1962. 1964 enterprise councils with worker/peasant representation numbered allegedly 657 and 2700 seats according to later survey; original worker-management study Panglaykim 1965 read only as abstract. [S199;S203; C234–C236]

1963 28 Nov 1963: Indonesian Act14 approved distinct Caltex/Pertamin, Shell/Permigan and Stanvac/Permina oil contracts of work, replacing colonial concession arrangements without automatically ending foreign operational management. [S197;S198; C222;C225]

1957 1957–65: Shell, Unilever and Heineken tried to maintain multinational status by registering subsidiary shareholdings through Canada, UK and Belgium/Luxembourg, and used expatriate/local management transfer mechanisms. Unilever factories could be under workers' and government supervision in 1964 while legal ownership remained foreign; not formal nationalization. [S204;C243-C245]

1965 1 Nov 1965（地点纠错）： 美国驻雅加达使馆第1290号电报开篇明确写明劳工专员于10月28—29日访问南苏门答腊巨港（Palembang, S Sumatra），并报告Shell、Stanvac炼厂及PUSRI化肥厂的PERBUM/SOBSI干部遭拘押。馆藏2017年编辑摘要误写North Sumatra，不能据此归入北苏门答腊；约600人是当地传闻数字，厂级约30/24/15人为不同范围的报告，不得相加或视为死亡人数。电报还记载Shell管理层反驳军方对Pladju火灾的破坏活动解释，Stanvac转移千名工人的方案只是考虑。已核电报OCR，尚未核扫描影像、军警拘押令及企业名册。 [S196;C229;C810;C1468;C1469] https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/15705/ocr

1965 7 Dec 1965: U.S. Embassy A-373 reports confiscation of money/property from Chinese-origin people and planned Army takeover of Baperki-linked rice mills/textile businesses. Policy report is not a verified ledger of specific firms. [S195;C230]

1965 21–27 Dec 1965: Surabaya consul described sealing textile wholesale center, rice shortages and employers' reported switch from SOBSI membership to SARBUMUSI affiliation/Army-veteran status as hiring qualification; geographically narrow field observation. [S152;C231-C233]

1965 30–31 Dec 1965: World Bank archive debt schedule dates Shell agreement effective Dec30 (US$110m with US$11m down and yearly principal instalments to 1971), while UN and historians date completed asset transfer Dec31. These are not inconsistent if signing and closing dates differ. [S205;S197;S204;C221;C242]

1966 17–18 Apr 1966: U.S. National Security Council memorandum confirms Indonesia signed a Title IV PL480 contract for 50,000 tons rice on Apr17, distinct from Feb Catholic Relief proposed 50,000 tons annually and from actual commodity receipt. [S201;S179;C240]

1966 1966–67: Enterprise councils reportedly ended, union organization moved from SOKSI to Sekber GOLKAR and civil-service/enterprise screening widened. Need original enterprise rosters before defining worker control elimination at a specific company. [S199;C237]

1967 1967–68: International labor complaint Case537 from World Federation of Trade Unions began Sep21 1967 over Indonesian trade-union arrests and SOBSI, formal government response Apr1968. Case outcome and individuals separate from complainants' allegations. [S206;C248]

1968 28 Aug 1968: WB industry review dates Permina/Pertamin merger into Pertamina, a separate event from 1965 oil asset transaction. [S202;C228]

1970 Jan 1970: UN record says Stanvac Sungai Gerong refinery and Tanjung Uban sea terminal were sold for US$4.75m and accompanying US$2m interest-free loan; WB archival folder describes another larger Stanvac liability, whose contract scope remains to determine. [S197;S205;C226]

1971 1971–72: SARBUMUSI lodged a union-freedom complaint with ILO June1971 (Case674), government denied the allegations, and committee suspended its review without merits finding. Different political source and later moment than the WFTU/SOBSI case 537. [S200;S206;C238]

1973 1973: ICJ retrospective dates FBSI government-led labor federation creation Feb20 and HKTI peasant consolidation Apr26, representing a subsequent institutional reorganization rather than direct administrative replacement on Oct1 1965. [S199;C239]

Important unresolved source divergence: UN A/8058 describes the US$12.9m marketing-asset transfer in 1965 while WB 1971 petroleum annex says 1964; detailed actual sale/registrations must determine asset-specific transfer dates. NSA curator narrative of refinery deals 'scuttled later in 1965' cannot be used as a blanket claim: Shell sold on Dec31 1965; Stanvac refinery remained private until Jan1970. [S196;S197;S202;C223;C241]

New deep-search: West Irian 1962-69 and post-1965 detentions (2026-11-08)

1962 15 Aug/21 Sep 1962: UNTS treaty records transfer from Dutch administration through UNTEA, Apr–May1963 control change, Article XVIII adult franchise/choice and XXII freedom guarantees. [S220; C277-C279]

1963 1963/Jan 1965: UN SG says repeated efforts to deploy UN experts were unsuccessful; Indonesia suspended UN co-operation Jan7 1965. [S221; C286]

1968 Aug–Nov 1968: Ortiz-Sanz arrives Jayapura, forwards guarantees of political rights and urges individual ballot in urban centers where practical. [S221; C280-C281]

1968 Dec 1968–Jul 1969: envoy receives govt releases correspondence reporting 346 political detainees released before consultation, including 115 from estates in West Java, individual lists still unread. [S221; C285]

1969 14 Jul–2 Aug 1969: official UN Secretary General reports 1,026 delegates at 8 regional consultations unanimously declared to remain with Indonesia. It does not report a general adult secret ballot. [S221; C282]

1969 19 Nov 1969: UNGA 1813 Gabon delegate raises objections on public voting/selection under government control; 2504 (XXIV) passes 84 for/0 against/30 abstain, 'takes note' of UN Secretary General record. [S222; C287-C288]

1966 12 Mar 1966: English translation of Suharto-signed presidential PKI dissolution/ban decision 1/3/1966 reproduced via 2016 tribunal and Dinuth; official signed original outstanding. [S224; C294]

1968 1968–69: ABC category screening state regulations KEP-028/10/68 amended by KEP-010/03/69, later than first killings and applying to civil/military public service. [S223;S224;C296]

1969 1969–1977: Amnesty documents Buru forced-labor detention, 1975 official regional category B table (29,470 nationally), Central Java local site countercounts and 1976 release-plus-transmigration announcement. Not a verified national census. [S223; C289-C293]

1970 1970–72: US original intelligence memo claims secret procurement of formerly Soviet Indonesian arms/radar, about $2m and return-aircraft logistics; prospective MiG sale not established. [S225; C298-C299]
