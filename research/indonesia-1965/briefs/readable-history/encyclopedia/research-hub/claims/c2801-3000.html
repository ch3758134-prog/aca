C2889 · 1965–1966; Central Java and national victim estimates · Data-scope finding and limit on ethnic-percentage inference High that these sources do not provide a victim-level ethnic distribution; no reliable national victim-level ethnic breakdown was located in this search.

The Central Java census reconstruction does not supply a national or regional percentage distribution of victims by ethnicity. Its data are population totals by administrative area plus election/geographic variables, not individual victim identity records; population gaps can also reflect refuge-seeking and other movement.
