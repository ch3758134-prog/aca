- Rewang’s later account says no start date had been set and a decision required provincial-committee approval; he says the movement was still preparing when Trisula arrived.

## Source-specific cautions

The U.S. Embassy itself says it had no collateral information confirming that local “revenge killings” were the work of a cohesive PKI group. Its mention of local religious/social tensions and soldier criminality is an alternative explanation, not proof of who committed any particular killing. The TNI report's detailed roster and military strategy are allegations from an intelligence process, not a complete party archive. Translation/OCR inconsistencies include PERDJUTA/PERMUTA and GRMAS/GERMAS.
