## Full-text verified
Vannessa Hearman, “Sweet Potato Dreaming: Development, Displacement and Food Crisis in South Blitar, East Java, 1968,” *Global Food History* 1(1), 2015, pp.81–102, DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/20549547.2015.11435413 . Author-uploaded full text opened and read at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/321323804_Sweet_Potato_Dreaming_Development_Displacement_and_Food_Crisis_in_South_Blitar_East_Java_1968 (2026-11-19). The journal's landing page alone was abstract-level, but this copy has text and notes.

The article pp.81–82 recounts Zainab's 2005-12-10 Lakpesdam NU interview: civilians evacuated to Bakung holding camps, ordered to bring food despite official promises of kitchens; severe hunger led people to consume sweet potatoes and leaves. This is a retrospective individual oral account, not a population-wide tally. Pp.92–95: military counterinsurgency restricted food and forced evacuation disrupted crops; the study cites military's own urgent food recommendation, *Semdam VIII Brawidjaja, Operasi Trisula*, p.162 (not yet directly retrieved). Local authorities supplied subsidized rice; roads/trade and cash-crop changes were promoted. Pp.94–95 mention some families returning to pre-1968 land after 1998. These are differentiated claims (interview, army document as mediated by historian, historian's interpretation), not one primary record.

The article's note 72 identifies a specific missing contemporaneous U.S. source: Department of State Airgram, “Inspection Trip to South Blitar,” Surabaya A-12, 1969-06-29, NARA POL18 INDON XR ORG 7, p.2; note 65 cites p.5. Note 64 cites Team Survey Dinas Disorganisasi Sosial, “Laporan Survey,” p.25; note 75 Markus Talam interview 2009-02-10. Notes 1,59–61 Zainab interview 2005-12-10. Note 7 Rewang interview Solo 2009-02-14. These are **source leads only** until originals obtained.
